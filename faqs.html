Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the portal, registration, academic attendance, and semester grade records.

Navigate to the homepage and select Register New Student under Student Access. Fill in your full name, student ID / roll number, university email, and mobile number. Once registered, you can log in directly.

Attendance is tracked per enrolled course subject based on total classes conducted versus classes attended. A minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory for university examination eligibility.

Log in to your student account, go to the Exam Result section in the dashboard sidebar, and view your semester course marks, GPA, and distinction status.

Access the Fee Information page from the dashboard menu to see your current tuition fee balance, semester breakdown, and download payment confirmation receipts.

You can submit an inquiry through our Contact Us page or email the campus IT Helpdesk at itdesk@charusat.ac.in during office hours (9:00 AM - 5:00 PM).
