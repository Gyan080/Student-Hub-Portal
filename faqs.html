Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers for students and staff. If you need more help, use the Contact page.

Question Answer
How do I register as a student? You can register by selecting the Register link on the homepage and completing the student form.
How do I reset my password? Use the Login page and follow the password recovery instructions or contact support for assistance.
Where can I view my courses? After logging in, your dashboard lists current courses and schedules.
Who do I contact for technical help? Contact the portal support team via the Contact page for technical assistance.
How do I update my profile information? Log in and go to your profile settings to update personal and contact details.
